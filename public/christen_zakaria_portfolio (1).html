A little about me

Strong foundations.
Confident thinkers.

With over five years of teaching experience across international curricula, I focus on making Chemistry structured, meaningful and accessible. My lessons are designed to help students connect scientific ideas, ask better questions and apply what they know with confidence.

From building foundational knowledge to preparing for advanced examinations, the aim stays the same: make learning clear, purposeful and active.

Curiosity + Clarity + Practice = ConfidenceA simple expression of the learning experience I aim to create in every Chemistry lesson.
The learning experience

Not just learning Chemistry.
Learning how to think.

A clear learning sequence that moves from understanding an idea to applying it independently.

01

Understand

Break complex concepts into clear, manageable explanations and connect them to scientific ideas.

02

Practise

Build accuracy through guided examples, targeted questions and deliberate practice.

03

Apply

Use knowledge in unfamiliar contexts, practical situations and exam-style questions.

04

Reflect & grow

Use feedback to recognise strengths, address gaps and make the next learning step clear.
